### Is this proactive or reactive?

**INFERENCE:** Mixed. The evidence shows roughly equal splits:
- **Proactive** buyers upgrade existing tents (Gear Aid reviews for visibility, tentgear360.co.uk "safety upgrade" framing, festival preparation)
- **Reactive** buyers replace degraded guylines (BPL forum: "needs new guylines," "I've replaced it on all my TTs")
- **New-tent buyers** get guylines bundled (tents come with basic guylines; upgrades are separate purchases)

| Option | Evidence | Assessment |

| **(A) Buy more tensioners (consumable repeat)** | **NO EVIDENCE FOUND.** No review or forum post says "tensioner broke again" or "I bought another set of tensioners." The absence is notable. | UNLIKELY — no evidence of this behavior |
| **(B) Buy a different tent accessory** | Some evidence: customers buying different tent accessories (stakes, poles, tarps) when upgrading gear. But no direct link between tensioner breakage and buying different accessories. | POSSIBLE but not evidenced |
