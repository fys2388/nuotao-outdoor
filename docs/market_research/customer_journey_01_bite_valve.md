## 6. Customer Journey Construction

### FIRST PURCHASE: How it starts

**Trigger:**
- Bite valve hardens, cracks, won't seal, smells bad, leaks, or breaks
- Often discovered mid-adventure (trail/cycling/running)
- Customer thinks: "My bladder is ruined!" — but the bladder is usually fine

**Product bought (most common):**
- **Option A (most common):** Universal replacement from Amazon — Aquatic Way 4-pack ($10.77) or Samorillo 5-pack ($8.99)
- **Option B (if brand-loyal):** CamelBak Big Bite Valve ($7.99) or brand-specific replacement
- **Option C (if Osprey user):** Wait 6-8 weeks for free Osprey replacement
- **Option D (if Montbell/niche brand):** Search for specific brand replacement (harder to find)

**Price sensitivity:** High — customers are comparing $5-15 options and will go with the cheapest/convenient

**Decision factors:**
1. Price (lowest price wins for most)
2. Compatibility (does it fit my bladder?)
3. Quantity (multi-pack = better value)
4. Reviews (4.5+ stars preferred)
5. Amazon Prime delivery speed
